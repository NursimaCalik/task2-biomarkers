**Do the measurement batches appear different? There were expected values at both batch so means of treatment group values were higher. means of control group and treatment group were close to each other for both batch. In the measurement batches, different numbers of male and female individuals were used for two different groups. This difference caused a slight variation in mean of measurements between batches. For instance , Batch 1 had 7 male for treatment group and Batch 2 had 2 male / 6 Female for treatment group. If the majority of male in Batch 1 for treatment group , small difference may make sense.In the given example, the ages of the male and female were also similar. The salient point of standard deviation was that standard deviation of treatment group in Batch 1 was a little bit low. Because this group consisted of males who were in similar ages. As a result, since the groups in the batches contained different numbers of male and female, the mean of the measurement values increased or decreased between batches to achieve balance. If the distribution of male and female numbers had been more balanced, it would have been clearer that the batches could have been different.

Interpretation

When comparing the results of female and male in different groups and of similar ages, the values were slightly higher in the treatment group, regardless of age. In fact, it was observed that the values were still slightly higher in the treatment group, even among individuals who were younger than those in the control group. However, when examining the effect of sex, it was notable that the average age of female in the control group was significantly lower than that of female in the treatment group, and their biomarker values were also markedly lower. If age had no effect, it would expect the difference between the groups to be smaller. Similarly, looking at the values in the table, the difference in measurements between the control and treatment groups increased as age decreased for both sex. In this case, it may not be said that there is a definitive link between age and gender. There were also some confusing aspects in the study. For example, the age distribution by sex was not balanced across the groups. The average age of female in the control group was significantly lower. Although there was no significant difference for male, there was a slight difference. The same situation applied across the batches. Furthermore, while the number of individuals in the control and treatment groups within each batch was not exactly the same, it was close however, the gender distribution within the groups was not balanced. For example, there were only 7 male individuals in the B1 treatment group. Apart from what was mentioned, there were no outliers or unusual observations. In any case, it would have been impossible to make a definitive interpretation without knowing the expected range for the biomarker. Before reaching more definitive conclusions, it might be necessary to know what the biomarker for the disease is, its normal range in the body, whether its levels can increase or decrease due to factors unrelated to the disease, or whether individuals are taking medication regularly. It may also be important to know individuals’ emotional state in daily life for example, whether they are under a great deal of stress.
